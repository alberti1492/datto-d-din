1618 Rounded and 1618 Mono · 1.100
1618 design and adaptation author: Matthias A. Peterseim.

1618 Mono now includes Thin, Light, Regular, Roman, Medium, Semibold,
Bold and Extrabold, each with a matching 11° oblique. Spacing glyphs
use a 620-unit advance at 1000 UPM. Combining marks and invisible
controls can have zero advance. The fixed-width design includes a
slashed zero and distinct I, l and 1.

1618 Rounded opens the letter and numeral spacing in Semibold, Bold
and Extrabold and their obliques by 18, 34 and 46 units respectively.
The other cuts retain their previous spacing.

Both families derive from Datto D-DIN Regular, designed by Charles Nix
at Monotype, with additional outlines from the Noto font project.

Complex-script shaping is limited and requires companion fonts.
