1618 Rounded 1.001 and 1618 Mono Regular 1.000

1618 design and adaptation author: Matthias A. Peterseim.

1618 Rounded renames 1618 Instruments Rounded 1.000. All sixteen styles
retain their original outlines, advances and layout tables.

1618 Mono Regular is derived from 1618 Rounded Regular. Spacing glyphs
have a 620-unit advance on a 1,000-unit em. Combining marks and layout
controls can have zero advance. I, i, l and 1 have typewriter-style
details, and zero has a slash. Wide glyphs are fitted to the cell.
Kerning and substitutions were omitted to preserve grid alignment.
This is a single Regular cut. Full shaping for complex scripts is
outside the coverage of this build.

Both families derive from Datto D-DIN and the Noto font project.
